About us

32+ years of composite engineering

Thermoset Poly Products (I) Pvt. Ltd. — an ISO 9001:2015 design & manufacturing company in Navi Mumbai, making the THERMODRAIN range of FRP/GRP/composite covers, gratings, gully covers, chambers and drain channels since 1993.

Backed by a strong design, R&D and technical team, we engineer covers and gratings for sewerage, electrical, telecom and cable networks — built to outperform cast iron, ductile iron and RCC in strength-to-weight, corrosion resistance and service life.

Our in-house testing facility runs one of the largest load-testing machines in the segment, rated to 90 tons, and every product is tested to BS EN 124, IS 1726 and AASHTO M306. A 250-strong team and a 40,000 sq ft manufacturing facility (over a 1,60,000 sq ft factory area) support a growing export presence worldwide.

Beyond drainage, our R&D has produced FRP windmill nacelle covers for the wind-power industry and MECHWOOD — a man-made artificial wood with zero wooden ingredients. An in-house CNC machining shop and stringent third-party testing keep quality consistent across the range.

Why FRP / GRP

Built to outlast iron & concrete

FRP is a thermoset composite — flexible, corrosion-free and non-reprocessable — engineered for the harshest drainage and utility environments.

Theft-proof

No metal and no scrap value — and a non-reprocessable thermoset — so covers are never stolen for resale.

Non-brittle, high impact

Flexible under excess load, so covers don't shatter like cast iron — chambers stay closed and roads stay safe.

Corrosion-free

No effect from sea water, sewage or chemical water. No rust, no algae, no protective GRP plate needed.

High strength-to-weight

Far lighter than iron or concrete — easy to handle, with lower freight and installation cost.

UV-stable & self-pigmented

UV-stabilised granite finish (or any colour) moulded in — never needs painting and won't fade or absorb heat.

Double seal & closed keyholes

Double-seal arrangement with bottom-closed lifting sockets — no foul smell and no mosquito breeding in chambers.

High-abrasion surface

A 7 mm high-abrasion top layer with stainless / HSPA lifting inserts resists traffic wear and repeated lifting.

Tested up to F900 (90 T)

Load-tested to BS EN 124, IS 1726 & AASHTO from 2.5 T up to F900 — with zero permanent set.

Thermoset chemistry

Cures at a 175 °C exothermic temperature into a thermoset — non-reversible, can't be melted or reprocessed. It resists theft, won't soften in extreme sun, and performs in extreme hot & cold.

The THERMODRAIN keyhole

Every cover lifts via a rod-type or keyhole-type arrangement, with lifting sockets moulded in High-Strength Plastic Alloy (HSPA) or stainless steel — and every keyhole is closed at the bottom, not through-and-through.

THERMODRAIN socket keyhole — with insert

  • HSPA / stainless-steel socket protects the FRP from wear during repeated lifting.
  • Bottom-closed — no foul smell escapes from the chamber.
  • Foreign particles can't drop into the chamber.
  • No standing water, so no mosquito breeding inside the chamber.
  • Keyhole area stays intact after many lifting operations.

Ordinary keyhole — no insert

  • Bare FRP wears and cracks around the keyhole with repeated lifting.
  • Open / through keyholes let foul smell escape.
  • Debris and foreign particles fall into the chamber.
  • Standing water in open holes invites mosquito breeding.

Load testing & permanent set
Every THERMODRAIN product is tested to BS EN 124 all the way up to load class F900, on one of the largest load-testing machines in the segment — rated to 90 tons (900 kN). Permanent set is a multiple-loading test that measures any permanent deformation in the cover after repeated loading within a short time, as specified in BS EN 124. Permanent set is zero across all THERMODRAIN sizes and load classes.

Do's

  • Install the frame correctly with complete civil support.
  • Apply load on the frame only after grouting is completed.
  • Handle the cover with care during transport and fitting.

Don'ts

  • Don't apply load before the grouting has fully set.
  • Don't throw or drop the material.
  • Don't skip the civil support around the frame.

How to choose your size
  1. Set the Length and Width — type an exact size (48–2400 mm), or use −/+ to grow the panel 10 mm at a time. An in-between size is cut to fit and shows open teeth on the growing edge until it reaches the next standard size.
  2. Set the bar height (30 mm or 40 mm) — taller bars carry heavier loads and longer spans.
  3. The edges are finished to your size. A standard moulded size gives a closed / flush edge; an in-between size is cut through the bars, so that edge shows an open / tooth edge that grows a little at a time until the next standard row completes. The preview redraws so you can see the exact edge for your size.
